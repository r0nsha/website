## Do you even know what you're doing?

Thank you for asking—no, I definitely don't. _"Oh no, another AI blog post!"_ I hear you say. Nope. It's just an innocent little em-dash that got vandalized by the recent LLM trend.

Okay, now that we're on the same page, aka I don't know what I'm doing here and neither do you, but we both don't like the fact that good writing got destroyed by "recent trends" - what will this blog be about?
Well... I'm not sure, but we'll (as the royal "We", aka Me) figure it out as we go along. And as we already know, we do have a few ideas floating around in our mind, but for now I'll stick with quoting our about page (as of this writing):

> "This is a place where I post up my various half-baked ideas and opinions about programming, work and life."

As for this post, I figured I'll write some interesting bits about my experience writing this website.
